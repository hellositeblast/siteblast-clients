What We Do

Our Concreting Services

From decorative driveways to commercial slabs, Brisbane Concreter handles every concrete project with care, precision and quality materials.

🚗

Concrete Driveways

Transform your property's entrance with a professionally laid concrete driveway. We offer plain, exposed aggregate, stencilled and coloured finishes to suit every style and budget.

🏗

Concrete Slabs

House slabs, extensions, shed bases and garage floors - our reinforced concrete slabs are engineered for load-bearing strength and long-term durability in Brisbane's climate.

✨

Exposed Aggregate Concrete

One of Brisbane's most popular decorative finishes - exposed aggregate reveals natural stone for a textured, slip-resistant and visually stunning surface for driveways and paths.

🛣

Concrete Paths and Pathways

Neat, durable concrete pathways that complement your home or commercial property. We finish edges cleanly and match your existing concrete where possible.

🌞

Concrete Patios and Entertaining Areas

Extend your living space outdoors with a beautifully finished concrete patio. Smooth, broomed or decorative - perfect for Brisbane's outdoor lifestyle all year round.

🏈

Concrete Pool Surrounds

Create a safe, stylish pool surround with slip-resistant concrete finishes. We work around your existing landscaping to deliver a seamless, professional result.

🏠

Shed and Garage Slabs

A properly prepared and poured slab is the foundation of any shed or garage. We laser-level every pour for a flat, even finish that will serve you for decades.

🏭

Decorative Concrete

Stencilled, stamped, coloured and patterned concrete finishes that turn functional surfaces into design features. Great for commercial spaces, feature walls and indoor floors.

🧱

Retaining Walls

Concrete block and poured retaining walls for sloped blocks, garden edges and structural applications. Designed to hold, built to last across Brisbane's varying terrain.

🔧

Concrete Resurfacing

Breathe new life into tired, cracked or stained concrete with professional resurfacing. A cost-effective alternative to full replacement that delivers a like-new result.
