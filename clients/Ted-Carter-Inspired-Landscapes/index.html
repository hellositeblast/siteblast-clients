What We Do

Our Services

From the first conversation to the final stone placed, Ted Carter Inspired Landscapes handles every stage of your outdoor transformation.

💬

Landscape Consultation

Every project begins with a conversation. Our consultation process gets to the core of how you want to live outdoors - what you want the space to feel like, how you want to use it, and what the property's natural character suggests. This is where the direction of the entire design is established, before a single plan is drawn or a single stone is moved. We work with discerning clients who want a landscape built around their property, not a catalogue of standard options.

✎

Landscape Design

Our design work produces a detailed concept tailored to your specific property and vision. Rather than reusing templates, each design responds to the site's topography, existing vegetation, natural light, and the owner's intended use. Natural stone features, spatial flow, planting composition, and seasonal interest are all considered within the one design document - so when the build begins, every decision has already been thought through in advance.

🏗

Landscape Installation

Installation is where the design becomes real. Ted Carter Inspired Landscapes specializes in working with natural stone - including stone stairs, walls, patios, and pathways - and transforming outdoor spaces that have sat underused or unconsidered. Our team brings the same attention to craft during the build phase that shaped the design, so the finished landscape matches the original vision rather than a compromised version of it.

📋

Project Management

As a design-build company, we oversee the full project lifecycle - from coordinating site preparation and material delivery through to scheduling, quality control, and final walkthrough. Clients working with Ted Carter Inspired Landscapes have one point of contact from start to finish, which means no miscommunication between a separate designer and builder, and no surprises when the project reaches the ground. The design-build model exists because it produces better outcomes.

About Us

Bespoke Landscapes for Discerning Clients

Ted Carter Inspired Landscapes offers landscape design and construction for discerning clients desiring a bespoke landscape for their property. The focus is on working with natural stone and transforming outdoor spaces that deserve more than a standard solution.

We are a design-build company - which means the same team that designs your landscape builds it. There is no hand-off between a design firm and a separate contractor, no gap between what was planned and what gets constructed. Serving southern Maine and coastal New Hampshire, our projects range across Cumberland and York counties, from Brunswick and Portland through to Kennebunkport, York, and Kittery.
