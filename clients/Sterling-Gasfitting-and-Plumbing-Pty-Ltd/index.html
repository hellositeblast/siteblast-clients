About Sterling

Family-Owned.
UK and Australian Trained.

Sterling Gasfitting & Plumbing is run by Jay Lennox -- a qualified gasfitter, plumber, and drainer who began his trade in the United Kingdom, where he completed a Certificate 3 in industrial and commercial plumbing and a heating engineering apprenticeship covering hydronic heating systems, boiler installation and commissioning, and the strict gas compliance regime the UK demands.

Back in Australia, Jay added a Certificate 4 in gasfitting and plumbing and built out a business with over 18 years of hands-on industry experience -- working across residential, commercial, and strata properties. The result is a genuinely rare combination: European technical depth applied to Australian conditions and standards.
