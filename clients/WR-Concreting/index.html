What We Do

Our Services

From new driveways and slabs to decorative finishes and repairs, WR Concreting handles every aspect of residential and commercial concreting across Melbourne.

🚗

Concrete Driveways

Transform your home's entrance with a durable, professionally laid concrete driveway. WR Concreting offers plain, coloured, and exposed aggregate finishes to suit every Melbourne property and budget.

⛶️

Concrete Slabs

We pour reinforced concrete slabs for garages, sheds, home extensions, alfresco areas and more. Every slab is properly prepared and compacted for long-lasting performance across Melbourne's varying soil conditions.

✨

Exposed Aggregate Concrete

Exposed aggregate is one of Melbourne's most popular driveway and path finishes. Attractive, slip-resistant, and low maintenance - WR Concreting delivers stunning results that enhance any property.

📶

Concrete Paths and Walkways

Well-designed concrete paths improve safety and kerb appeal. WR Concreting lays neat, durable paths and walkways for homes and commercial properties throughout Melbourne and surrounding suburbs.

🎨

Coloured Concrete

Add personality to your outdoor spaces with custom coloured concrete. WR Concreting offers a wide range of integral and surface colours to complement your home's architecture and landscaping.

🧱

Concrete Retaining Walls

Structurally sound and built to last, our concrete retaining walls manage slopes and erosion while adding definition to your outdoor areas. Designed and constructed to meet Melbourne's site-specific requirements.

🏊

Concrete Pool Surrounds

Create a beautiful, safe pool environment with professionally finished concrete surrounds. WR Concreting uses slip-resistant, durable finishes designed to withstand Melbourne's weather and constant exposure to water.

🔨

Concrete Repairs

Cracked, sunken, or damaged concrete? WR Concreting provides expert repair solutions that restore both the function and appearance of your concrete surfaces at a fraction of full replacement cost.
