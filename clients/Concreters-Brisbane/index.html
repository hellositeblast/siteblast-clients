What We Do

Our Concreting Services

From residential driveways to commercial slabs - Concreters Brisbane delivers quality concrete solutions across Greater Brisbane.

🚘

Concrete Driveways

Transform your home's kerb appeal with a durable, professionally finished concrete driveway. We offer plain, brushed, and exposed aggregate finishes to suit any home style and budget.

🏗

Concrete Slabs

Structural concrete slabs for homes, extensions, sheds, and commercial applications. Our slabs are engineered for long-term performance in Brisbane's climate and soil conditions.

✨

Exposed Aggregate Concrete

Exposed aggregate is one of Brisbane's most popular concrete finishes - slip resistant, attractive, and incredibly durable. Perfect for driveways, paths, and pool surrounds.

🛹

Concrete Paths and Pathways

Safe, smooth concrete paths that enhance the functionality and look of your property. We install garden paths, footpaths, and pedestrian walkways for residential and commercial clients.

🏈

Concrete Pool Surrounds

Complement your pool with a professionally finished concrete surround. We specialise in slip-resistant, heat-reduced finishes that stand up to Brisbane's sun and heavy use.

🌟

Decorative Concrete

Elevate your outdoor spaces with stamped, coloured, or patterned decorative concrete. A cost-effective way to achieve the look of premium paving with the strength and longevity of concrete.

🧱

Concrete Retaining Walls

Prevent soil erosion and create defined garden levels with reinforced concrete retaining walls. Our team handles engineering, formwork, and pour for walls of all heights and lengths.

🏠

Shed and Garage Slabs

A solid, level concrete slab is the foundation of any great shed or garage. Concreters Brisbane gets the prep and pour right the first time, with correct drainage and reinforcement.

🔧

Concrete Repairs

Cracked, sunken, or damaged concrete? Our team provides expert concrete repair and resurfacing to restore the look and structural integrity of your existing concrete surfaces.
