About Us

Local Lawn & Garden Experts You Can Count On

Yard n Home is a Toowoomba-based lawn and garden care business built on one simple idea: your outdoor space should be something you're proud of. We provide professional lawn mowing, garden maintenance, hedging, landscaping, and yard cleanups for both homeowners and businesses throughout the Toowoomba region.

What sets us apart is our genuine care for the properties we look after and the people we work with. Every job - large or small - is completed with attention to detail and a focus on doing it right the first time.
