Our Rendering Services

From new builds to renovations, Sydney Renderers covers the full range of exterior and interior rendering needs.

🏗

Cement Rendering

Traditional sand-and-cement render applied by hand for a solid, durable base coat. Ideal for brick, block, and masonry surfaces across Sydney.

🌞

Acrylic Rendering

Flexible, crack-resistant acrylic systems that offer a smooth or textured finish. Weather-resistant and suited to Sydney's variable climate.

🌐

Texture Coating

Acrylic-based coatings applied over render to add colour, texture, and weather protection in a single application. Wide range of finishes available.

🔨

Polymer Rendering

High-performance polymer renders that bond strongly to the substrate, offering superior flexibility and longevity for residential and commercial projects.

⭐

Rockcote Rendering

Specialist application of Rockcote's premium render and coating systems. Trusted by builders and homeowners across Sydney for superior results.

🏠

Rendered Feature Walls

Interior and exterior feature walls finished with render for a modern, architectural look. A popular choice in contemporary Sydney homes and offices.
